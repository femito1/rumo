## 3. The one genuinely open piece of work

### Seguro de Responsabilidade Civil is in the wrong family, and the fix needs the extract

Adriana ruled that `020.060.0040` belongs in **Administrativas**, not Ocupação: *"não é um
seguro referente a ocupação, ele é do negócio."* The DB agrees — `nome_conta_pai` is already
`Administrativas`. Our `_CONTA3_TO_SECTION` override in `app/closing/workbook_layouts.py`
deliberately puts it in Ocupação, and that override is now known to be wrong.

**But do not just flip the override, and do not split it by value.** The account bundles TWO
seguros; the workbook splits them and we do not:

| | Jan | Fev–Jun |
|---|---|---|
| book r91 Seguro **Locação** (Ocupação) | 182,71 | 182,71 |
| book r133 Seguro **Resp. Civil** (Administrativas) | 2.539,84 | 0 |
| our single `020.060.0040` | **2.722,55** | **182,71** |

Three tempting rules, all measured and all wrong:

* **Subtract the recurring 182,71** — breaks on **2025-10 and 2025-11**, where the resp-civil
  posted ALONE (total 2.539,85, `n=1`). The rule would invent 2.357,14 of resp-civil and
  −182,71 of locação that never existed, silently, wrong in both families.
* **Discriminate on `n`** — `n=2` is usually locação+resp-civil but 2025-08/09 are also
  `n=2`; `n=1` is usually locação alone except those two months, where it is the reverse.
* **Hardcode the locação amount** — it already moved (182,75 in 2025, 182,71 in 2026) and
  Renata said the policy renews mid-year (apólice 20/06/25–20/06/26, Liberty, 7 instalments).

Flipping the override wholesale is also worse where it counts — measured over Jan–Jun:

| | Ocupação ties | Σ\|erro\| of both families |
|---|---|---|
| today | **2 of 6 months** | 4.308,03 |
| after flip | **0 of 6** | 3.483,93 |

The total error falls, but every month acquires a permanent ±182,71. Trading two exact ties
for six months of guaranteed error is the wrong trade for a document whose credibility rests
on June tying cleanly.

**The sound fix:** widen the extract so `020.060.0040` carries its individual lançamentos with
their `histórico` (Renata read "Liberty Seguros" and the apólice dates straight off her
screen, so the text exists in `CONTASPAGAR`). Then the split is READ, not inferred, and
survives renewals and odd months. `despesas_desdobramento` only covers unfolded slices, so
these direct postings need their own block or a widening of `despesas_liquido`.

⚠ **Probe the box before writing that SQL.** The v5 attempt (`HANDOFF_v5_reverted_2026-08-04.md`)
was reverted because a green local test modelled something the box does not do. Validate
against MBC-LDESK01 first.

**Money is unaffected meanwhile.** Both families feed `r198`, so the pair nets to zero in the
institucional total, the rateio pool and the Resultado Bruto. It is a presentation difference,
and the document already names the exact rows via the *Onde está o resto* column.

### Two traps in the document's own machinery

Both are guarded by `tests/test_diferencas_doc.py` (18 tests) and both cost real credibility
when they were live:

1. **Use the `_delta` helper** (`round(ours) − round(theirs)`), never `round(ours − theirs)`.
   The latter made Σ(months) ≠ Acumulado and printed **+3.140,19 in the summary and +3.140,20
   in the detail table** for the same line. The workbook carries trailing half-centavos.
2. **Materiality is R$ 1.000 on the YTD *or any single month*.** YTD-only hid *Econômico ·
   Despesas Equipe* (−31,45 YTD, but −1.166,75 in Feb and +1.504,72 in May) — exactly the
   netting artifact the document's own second paragraph warns about.

And one editorial rule with teeth: **a line-level delta is not an answer.** When a value is
missing from a family, say WHERE it went — the *Onde está o resto* column exists because
"Ocupação difere +2.539,84" was unanswerable until it named r133 in Administrativas.
`_onde_esta` / `_onde_esta_nosso` search both directions, including inside desdobramento
lines. Match leaves by **value, never by label** (our one *Serviços de Informática* is the
book's *Suporte de Informática* + *Suporte Totvs*).
